What PairDesk does not do

  • It does not execute anything, and it never will. It is a screen and a register.
  • It has no live feed. Standings are as of the build date, printed on every page.
  • It does not run your rules. If your PTL work was the backtester, this replaces the database half, not that half.
  • It covers US stocks and ETFs only. Not India, not crypto, not FX, not futures.
  • Its price source drops most names that stopped trading. The cost of that is measured and printed today; a survivorship-correct US panel is the next build.
How to judge it in ten minutes. Open the free build, type three pairs you knew from the PTL database into the register, and read their dossiers: the test in both directions, the walk-forward z, the half-life, the capacity, and the caveats that apply to that pair. If what you find is worse than what you had, you have lost ten minutes. If it is more honest, the seat prices are below.
