How fast a desk goes stale, measured

Two goods age at two speeds. The list (which pairs pass) moves slowly, because a few sessions against a 2,500-session window is nothing. The standings (which spreads sit beyond two sigma today) move with daily prices, and a standing that faded mostly faded because the spread converged: the opportunity played out on someone else's screen.
